# Future-State (To-Be) Clinical Workflow

## Project Overview

I developed a proposed future-state clinical workflow to improve how RN Case Managers prepare for in-home comprehensive nursing assessments.

The design addresses opportunities to strengthen preparation consistency, document accessibility, and workflow accountability while preserving nursing autonomy and patient-centered care.

Rather than completely redesigning the existing process, I focused on targeted improvements that could make clinical preparation more structured and reliable.

## Proposed Workflow Improvements

### 1. Standardized Preparation Checklist

Introduce a printable checklist to support consistent completion of essential pre-visit activities, including:

- Patient information and medical record review
- Retrieval of required assessment forms
- Equipment and supply preparation
- Technology readiness
- Appointment confirmation

### 2. Centralized Current Assessment Forms

Establish a consistently organized location for current assessment forms to simplify document retrieval and reduce uncertainty about document versions.

### 3. Visit Readiness Verification

Introduce a clearly defined preparation endpoint documented as **"Ready for Visit – Complete,"** with RN initials and the completion date and time.

This step occurs after required preparation activities and appointment confirmation have been completed.

## Workflow Design Principles

The proposed workflow maintains:

- RN Case Manager autonomy and clinical responsibilities
- Patient-centered appointment scheduling
- Existing supervisor escalation procedures
- Coordination between participating roles and systems
- Safety considerations before in-home visits

These principles guided the design of targeted improvements without introducing unnecessary changes to established clinical responsibilities.

## Expected Benefits

The proposed improvements are intended to:

- Reduce the risk of incomplete pre-visit preparation
- Improve access to current assessment documentation
- Strengthen accountability through documented readiness verification
- Support greater consistency in clinical workflow execution
- Improve coordination across workflow participants

## Project Status

**Proposed workflow design — not implemented.**

These improvements represent anticipated operational benefits. Implementation outcomes and performance measures have not been evaluated.
