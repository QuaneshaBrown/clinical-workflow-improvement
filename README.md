# Clinical Workflow Design & Process Improvement

### In-Home Comprehensive Nursing Assessment Preparation

## Project Overview

I developed a proposed future-state (To-Be) clinical workflow to improve the preparation process for in-home comprehensive nursing assessments.

Drawing on my clinical nursing experience and health informatics knowledge, I examined how RN Case Managers coordinate patient assignments, scheduling, documentation, and visit preparation.

My goal was to design a more structured and reliable workflow while preserving nursing autonomy and patient-centered care.

## Proposed Workflow Improvements

The proposed workflow incorporates three key improvements:

- **Standardized Preparation:** A checklist to help RN Case Managers consistently complete pre-visit preparation activities.
- **Improved Document Accessibility:** A centralized location for current assessment forms to support efficient document retrieval.
- **Readiness Verification:** A documented preparation-completion step to strengthen accountability before patient visits.

## Workflow Diagrams

I developed three workflow diagrams to illustrate the proposed process:

1. **Level 1 Handoff Diagram:** Provides an overview of the actors, systems, and major workflow activities.
2. **Level 2 Service Diagram — Part A:** Details assignment review, patient contact, scheduling, escalation, and form retrieval.
3. **Level 2 Service Diagram — Part B:** Details preparation, confirmation, readiness verification, travel, and safety.
